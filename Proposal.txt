Upwork Talent Accelerator: AI Chatbot Developer
AI Chatbot Development Invite Only Posted May 21, 2026
Description:
We’re looking for skilled chatbot developers who want to build their business reputation on Upwork by completing a simulated project. Based on the results of the simulated project, freelancers will receive feedback and a rating that can be viewed by clients on the platform.

The simulated project focuses on building a customer support chatbot that could be used by a small e-commerce business selling outdoor apparel and camping gear. All business context, FAQs, and mock data will be provided so you can complete the project output independently. Your deliverables may be used to train Upwork and/or third-party AI models.

Scope of Work
Through this simulated project, freelancers will be asked to:
Build a chatbot that handles common customer support scenarios
Implement key use cases including:
Order tracking
Returns and exchanges
Product recommendations
Human handoff
Design clear, user-friendly conversation flows

Eligibility Requirements:
In order to be eligible for this simulated project, freelancers must have:
Experience with chatbot tools (e.g., Botpress, Voiceflow, Diaglogflow) or custom solutions
Ability to design and implement conversational flows
Strong understanding of customer support interactions

Deliverables:
Freelancers will generate the following outputs in the project:
Working chatbot (export file or code repository)
Short video demo (203 minutes) showing core use cases
